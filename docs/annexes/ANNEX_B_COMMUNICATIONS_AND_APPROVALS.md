We, the undersigned fourth-year BSIT students from Partido State University, are currently enrolled in CAP2 – Capstone and Research 2. As part of our requirements, we are in the process of developing our capstone project and are currently in the requirements-gathering phase.

Our capstone project is titled "AURORA: Paperless Academic Defense Workflow System for Research, Capstone, Thesis & Dissertation Papers at Partido State University." This system is designed to digitize and streamline the entire academic defense process, from manuscript submission to the final evaluation. We believe this project will be a significant step toward a more efficient, organized, and paperless workflow for all colleges within the university.

The specific objectives of our project are to:
1. Gather requirements by identifying functional and non-functional needs for paperless defense workflows across research, capstone, and dissertation papers.
2. Design the system architecture, database schema, user interface, and workflows that support manuscript submission, annotation, rubric-based evaluation, and revision tracking.
3. Develop the academic defense workflow system by integrating role-based access control, annotation workspace, rubric-based assessment, and automated scoring modules.
4. Evaluate the system's usability, efficiency, and acceptance using standardized instruments such as the System Usability Scale (SUS) and UTAUT-based acceptance surveys.
